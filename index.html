Pest Control in South Lebanon

The best pest control
in Saidoun & beyond.

Homes and businesses across Saidoun, Jezzine and the South trust Agri Pro Services for pest control that actually lasts — thorough, safe and built around your property.

We treat the full range of household and commercial pests — cockroaches, ants, rodents (rats and mice), bed bugs, fleas, mosquitoes, wasps and stored-product insects. Every job starts with a full inspection to find the source and entry points, followed by a targeted treatment and a prevention plan that keeps the problem from coming back.

Our products and methods are chosen to be safe for families, children and pets, and are applied by trained technicians who follow correct dosing and safety practice. For ongoing peace of mind we offer scheduled maintenance visits and rapid, same-region call-outs.
